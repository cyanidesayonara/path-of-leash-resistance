## 2026-11-20 - Els Encants: the canopy and the lanes

- The flea market's mirrored canopy now reads overhead: zig-zag underside facets, a warped reflection of the stalls below, steel edge beams, and a drip line when it rains over a visibly dry floor.
- Stalls pack both sides of the lane with junk heaps, striped awnings, sellers on folding chairs and browsers in the aisle; floor ramps cast their own slope shadow.
- The wet street at Glòries has reflections, umbrellas and a distant Torre Glòries shape (no branding); the auction ring and the tower of chairs are landmarks you can spot from across the lane.
- Plaça dels Encants at the finish is a paved square with arcade edges, not grass on grey; website shots retaken.
