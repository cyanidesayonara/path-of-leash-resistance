## 2026-11-27 - La Ciutadella: Arc crown and promenade palms

- The Passeig de Lluís Companys palms keep their kerb pits and now use the
  same fronded crown as the beach, instead of seven green sticks.
- The Arc de Triomf's gold discs are Barcelona's arms: a pointed shield
  quartered, Sant Jordi's red cross on white and the four red bars on gold,
  under a bat with scalloped wings over the stone frieze.
