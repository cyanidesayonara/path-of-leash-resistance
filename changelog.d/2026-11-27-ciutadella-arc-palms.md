## 2026-11-27 - La Ciutadella: Arc crown and promenade palms

- The Passeig de Lluís Companys palms keep their kerb pits and now use the
  same fronded crown as the beach, instead of seven green sticks.
- The Arc de Triomf's gold discs are Barcelona's shield and bat: Sant Jordi's
  cross, the four bars, and tapered wings over the stone frieze.
