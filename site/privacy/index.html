Privacy policy

Path of Leash Resistance collects nothing about you. Updated November 22, 2026.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or anything else, and it sends nothing anywhere. The Android version does not even ask for permission to use the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, bones, the walks you have unlocked, your dogs' training, what they wear, your human's look) and your settings (sound, language, controls, accessibility) are saved on your device only: in the app's own storage on Android and Windows, and in your browser's storage when you play on the web. Nobody else can see them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Where you get the game

The stores and sites you download or play the game from (Google Play, the Microsoft Store, itch.io and this website on GitHub Pages) run their own services under their own privacy policies, for example to handle your download or keep server logs. The game itself shares nothing with them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does, with the date at the top. Questions are welcome on the game's issue tracker or on its itch.io page.
